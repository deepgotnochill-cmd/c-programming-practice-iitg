A little more about this Pokédex.

Made by Deep Datta Chaudhury, this independent fan-made field guide makes it easy to explore Pokémon across all nine generations. Search by name or number, browse types, and open an entry for Pokédex descriptions, stats, abilities, evolutions, habitat, and general type matchups.

Photo lookup can open your camera or image library and preview your picture. It does not identify what is in a photo: no recognition model is configured. You can search the Pokédex manually instead; photos are not uploaded to a recognition service.
